01 — About

What I do

Summary

I am a customer service and technical support professional with over seven years of BPO and remote work experience. I resolve technical issues, manage social media accounts, and drive prospect outreach.

The through line is the same in every role: figure out what the person on the other end actually needs, then handle it without making them repeat themselves. That started on Amazon device support, where I troubleshot Kindles, Fire tablets and mobile apps over phone, chat and email. It carried into call analysis and prospecting at a Houston marketing firm, into book research and copy written for ghostwriters at an Australian publisher, and into the social accounts I now plan, edit and schedule for agencies in Germany.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Father Saturnino Urios University and communicate fluently in English at C2 level.
